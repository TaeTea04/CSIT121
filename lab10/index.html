What is WebRTC?

WebRTC stands for Web Real-Time Communication. It is a technology that allows people to communicate through video calls, voice calls, and sharing information directly through web browsers. It makes it easier for people to connect online without having to download extra software or plugins. WebRTC is an important part of how people communicate using technology today.

WebRTC allows people to communicate with each other in real time through websites and applications. It makes long-distance communication easier, especially for things like job interviews, online appointments, and virtual meetings. Instead of having to meet someone in person, people can communicate from almost anywhere with an internet connection. This makes everyday life more convenient and helps people stay connected.

WebRTC is used in many websites and applications that support online communication. Some examples include Google Meet and Discord, which use WebRTC for certain real-time communication features. Video calling applications like Zoom and FaceTime also show how useful real-time communication technology has become, although they do not always rely on WebRTC. Whether someone is attending an online class, having a virtual appointment, or talking with family, this type of technology makes communication easier.
