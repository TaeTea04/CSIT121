How is WebRTC Used?

WebRTC allows people to make video and voice calls directly through websites and web browsers. It lets users see and hear each other in real time without needing extra browser plugins. The process is usually quick and simple, making it easy to start conversations with others. This allows people to communicate smoothly, even when they are in different locations.

WebRTC can also be used to share screens and send information between users. Screen sharing allows people to show presentations, documents, or other things on their screens during online meetings. This is helpful for students working on group projects or employees giving presentations from different locations. WebRTC also supports sending data directly between users, making file sharing and collaboration easier.

WebRTC is used in applications like Google Meet and Discord to support real-time communication. These platforms use WebRTC technology for features such as voice calls, video calls, and sharing information. WebRTC can also be used for online tutoring, customer support, and virtual meetings where people need to communicate instantly. This technology helps these services connect people through their browsers without requiring everyone to be in the same place.
